About

I graduated from the University of British Columbia in May 2026 with a BASc in Electrical Engineering (First Class Honours). I like work where a measurement doesn't match what I expected and I have to find out why, whether that's a failed test, a board that won't regulate, or a thin film that comes out the wrong thickness.

Today I work in quality assurance at an aerospace manufacturer, investigating failures to root cause and supporting first article inspection. Before that I interned at EnerSys (Alpha Technologies), qualifying production test equipment and analyzing field returns of telecom power systems. The direction I'm building toward is semiconductors: test, validation, characterization and analog/mixed-signal design.

Outside engineering I play badminton and piano, read, and play video games (retired).
